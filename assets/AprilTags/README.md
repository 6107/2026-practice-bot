# 2026 Rebuilt April Tags

These are the April Tags used for the 2026 season. They are rebuilt
from scratch to be more accurate and easier to use. The tags are available
in both black and white versions, and can be printed on paper or other materials.
They were extracted from the official FRC 2026 game manual and are intended
for use in practice and testing environments.

In the official 2026 REBUILT field layout from WPILib, the tag heights run
from 21.75 in (minimum) to 44.25 in (maximum). These are heights to the tag center.

| Height to tag center | Tag IDs |
|---|---|
| 44.25 in | 2–5, 8–11, 18–21, 24–27 |
| 35 in | 1, 6, 7, 12, 17, 22, 23, 28 |
| 21.75 in | 13–16, 29–32 |


## Onshape Assemblies

I used these tags to create Onshape assemblies for the 2026 season. The assemblies 
are available from our 
[Team Room CAD folder](https://cad.onshape.com/documents/247480e5cb85c6d62032b498/w/cdf1e164ab43278f00f8c118/e/622dbe56df5ce2843c1aa85d?renderMode=0&uiState=6ae3588b1adf9291899eccb6)
in case you want to use them in your own designs. The assemblies include the tags in both
black and white versions, and can be easily imported into your own Onshape projects.
